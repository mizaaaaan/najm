ABOUT

A steady hand behind the paperwork

NAZMUL H. SUJAN
DOHA, QATAR

Nazmul keeps organisations running on time and on the record — from cash deposits to correspondence, in whichever of five languages the day calls for.

Originally from Noakhali, Bangladesh, Nazmul studied social science, Islamic culture & history, and political science at the National University of Bangladesh before moving into administration and accounts work in Doha. Since 2016 he has worked his way from front-desk reception to ledger entry, cash handling and office administration at Qatar Sports Club, adding a Professional Finance & Accounting course at Qatar University along the way.

Outside the office, he keeps a steady routine of reciting the Holy Qur'an, staying active, and exploring new cultures and languages when he travels.
